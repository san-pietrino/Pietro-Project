* In Italy, when you borrow something from a friend, they’ll smirk and say: "Si chiama Pietro... e torna indietro!" ("Its name is Pietro... and it comes back!"). It’s a playful, rhyming way of saying: "You can totally use my stuff... but I definitely want it back!" It turns an awkward reminder into a fun little joke.
